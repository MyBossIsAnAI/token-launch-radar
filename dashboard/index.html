Methodology — radar-2

Implied value = TVL × base multiple × growth × maturity. The base multiple is the median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of traded peers in the same category (at least four peers), else the category family median, else the whole-market median. Growth adjusts for the 7-day TVL change, capped at −60% / +120% and halved. Maturity rises from 0.6 for a new protocol to 1.0 after three years; unknown age counts as one year.
